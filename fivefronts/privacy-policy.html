Privacy policy

Five Fronts · Version 2026-10-04

Who is responsible

The controller of any personal data is TML-Consulting, Södermannagatan 34, Stockholm, Sweden. Contact (support and privacy): info@tml-consulting.se.

What the game stores

On your device only: your hero (name and look), progress, stars, stamina, sound settings and the age group you chose (we keep your age group, not your birth year). This stays in the app's storage on your device and is never sent to us.

The game has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. We do not receive any personal data from you.

If you write to info@tml-consulting.se, we use your email address and your message only to answer you.

Ads

The game can show optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one to get a small reward, such as extra stamina). They are provided by Google AdMob, which acts as an independent controller of the data it needs to show ads.

Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about the ad and your device to select and show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. We do not receive this data.

Players aged 13 to 15 get only non-personalised ads. If you say you are under 13, no ads are shown at all. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), we ask for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later under "Ad privacy choices" on your hero screen. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings.

Read how Google uses data from apps that use its ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Legal bases (GDPR article 6)

Ads: your consent (6.1.a) where the law requires it, otherwise the legitimate interest of Google AdMob and us in funding the game (6.1.f). Replying to your emails: legitimate interests (6.1.f).

Your rights

We hold no game data about you, so there is nothing for us to give, correct or delete. To remove everything the game stored, uninstall it or clear its data in your phone's settings. For data held by Google AdMob, use its privacy controls. You may ask us for a copy or deletion of any correspondence with us, and you can complain to the data protection authority: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), Sweden.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, children included. If you say you are under 13, ads are switched off. If you are under 16, only non-personalised ads can appear.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will tell you in the app before it applies. This version is dated 2026-10-04.
