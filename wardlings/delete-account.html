Wardlings · Version 2026-10-04

There is no account to delete

The game has no accounts. Your hero and progress are stored only on your device. To erase them, uninstall the game or clear its storage in your phone's settings (Settings, Apps, Wardlings, Storage, Clear data).

We hold no other data about you. If you have written to us and want that correspondence deleted, write to info@tml-consulting.se and we will confirm within one month.
